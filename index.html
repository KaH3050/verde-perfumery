    Our story

    Small batches,
    soft as air

    Verde started in a tiny Miami studio with one idea: perfume should feel like weather, light enough to live in and memorable enough to be missed. Every scent is blended in small batches with oils sourced from Grasse, then rested for weeks before bottling.

    No harsh alcohol bite, no overpowering trails. Just clean, long-lasting eaux de parfum that sit close to the skin and open up through the day.
